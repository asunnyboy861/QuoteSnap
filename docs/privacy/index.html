Privacy Policy

QuoteSnap — Last updated: April 26, 2026

QuoteSnap is designed with privacy first. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. All your quotes, invoices, and client information stay on your device.

Data We Collect

QuoteSnap does not collect any personal data from users. Specifically:

  • No personal information is transmitted to our servers
  • No analytics or tracking identifiers are collected
  • No usage data is shared with third parties
  • No advertising identifiers are used

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Business information (company name, logo, contact details)
  • Client information (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses)
  • Quotes and invoices (line items, pricing, notes)
  • Customer signatures (stored as image data)
  • App preferences and settings

This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

iCloud Sync (Pro Feature)

If you purchase QuoteSnap Pro and enable iCloud sync:

  • Data is synced via Apple's CloudKit infrastructure
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple
  • We do not have access to your synced data
  • You can disable sync at any time in Settings

In-App Purchases

QuoteSnap Pro is a one-time purchase processed through Apple's App Store. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive only a confirmation of purchase — no payment details are shared with us.

Third-Party Services

QuoteSnap does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual quotes, invoices, or clients within the app
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all local data
  • Managing iCloud data through your device's iCloud settings

Children's Privacy

QuoteSnap is a business tool intended for professional use. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
