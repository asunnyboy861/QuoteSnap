Frequently Asked Questions

How many quotes can I create for free?

The free tier allows you to create up to 3 quotes per month. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited quotes.

What's included in QuoteSnap Pro?

Pro includes: unlimited quotes, 8 industry templates, invoice conversion, custom branding with logo and colors, e-signature, and iCloud sync across devices. It's a one-time purchase — no subscription.

Can I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Yes! Your Pro purchase is linked to your Apple ID. Go to Settings → Restore Purchases on your new device.

How do I add my company logo to quotes?

Go to Settings → Company & Branding. Tap "Choose Logo" to upload your logo from your photo library. Your logo will appear on all generated PDFs.

Can I convert a quote to an invoice?

Yes, Pro users can convert any accepted quote to an invoice with one tap. The invoice retains all the quote details and gets a new invoice number.

Is my data backed up?

Pro users with iCloud sync enabled have their data automatically synced and backed up via iCloud. Free users should regularly export important quotes as PDFs.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed through Apple. To request a refund, go to your Apple ID purchase history or visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Quick Tips

  • Use industry templates to pre-fill common line items for faster quoting
  • Import client info directly from your Contacts app
  • Set your brand color in Company & Branding for professional-looking PDFs
  • Use the e-signature feature to get client sign-off on the spot
  • Share PDFs directly via email or AirDrop from the quote detail screen
