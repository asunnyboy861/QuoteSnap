Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Download QuoteSnap from the App Store and tap the "+" button to create your first quote. Fill in client details, add line items, and export as PDF.

What is included in the free version?

The free version includes 3 quotes per month, 1 general template, client management, and basic PDF export.

What do I get with QuoteSnap Pro?

QuoteSnap Pro ($9.99 one-time purchase) unlocks unlimited quotes, 8 industry templates, quote-to-invoice conversion, brand customization, electronic signature, CloudKit sync, and more.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open QuoteSnap, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases". Your Pro features will be restored automatically.

Can I use QuoteSnap on multiple devices?

Yes! With QuoteSnap Pro, you can enable CloudKit sync in Settings to sync your quotes and clients across all your Apple devices.

How do I share a quote?

Open a quote and tap the Share button. You can share via email, Messages, AirDrop, or save to Files.

Is my data secure?

All your data is stored locally on your device by default. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is encrypted and stored securely in your personal iCloud account.
